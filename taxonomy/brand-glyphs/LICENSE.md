# Brand glyphs

The SVG files in this folder are copied verbatim from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org), released under **CC0 1.0 Universal** (public domain). Each file carries the brand's own title.

The brand marks themselves are trademarks of their owners. FamilyHub shows a mark for one purpose only: to help a person recognise a charge they already made (recurring-charges-spec.md §3.3, RR22). It never implies partnership, endorsement or affiliation. A brand that asks is removed from `taxonomy/brands.json` and falls back to the category emoji in the next build.

Brands whose owners have asked Simple Icons to remove their marks (Adobe, Microsoft, LinkedIn, Disney+ among them) are deliberately absent here too.
